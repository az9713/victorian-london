### Fixlist item 2 — canopy re-built and simulated as cloth

between supports, and the r6 judge read it as "a zigzag sheet-metal or
folded-paper roof — dead-straight edges meeting at sharp angular peaks and
valleys, no curve anywhere." Rebuilt in `build_canopy()`/`sim_canopy_sheet()`:

columns and the 3 interior ridge-tie columns (everything else free), dropped
under gravity through Blender's Cloth solver, stepped frame-by-frame headless

evaluated depsgraph — no `bpy.ops.ptcache.bake*`), and the baked deformation is
what produces the sag — not an authored curve.

- **Catenary curve, not straight-line origami — PASS, countable in
  `stall_face.png`/`stall_34.png`.** Both frames show a continuous curved sag

  defining property r6 failed on. Verified directly: I looked at `stall_face.png`
  and traced the profile between the two end poles and the visible interior
  ridge point; it is a smooth arc, not two straight segments meeting at a point.
- **Uneven/rippled free edges — PASS, countable in `stall_face.png`.** The
  canopy's front edge shows small irregular undulations along its length rather

  camera this pass — see gap below) shows the bead as an unmistakably thick,
  round, brighter strip standing proud in front of the flat 0.018 m sheet plane

- **3 grommets, all visible in one frame — PASS, re-shot this pass,
  `stall_hem.png`.** Small proud iron cylinders (radius 0.020 m, height 0.014 m,

## sack-pile — sandbox/assets/models/sacks.glb — round-7 body re-simulated as cloth

**What it is:** a pile of 3 slumped sacks with tied, gathered necks, flat-ish
panel-like bodies, and irregular cloth folds and creases wherever the cloth
presses against its own contents, the ground, or another sack. The neck terminus
(pinch, cord-wrap collar, flared tied end) — passing since round 5 — was **not
touched** this round: the neck branch of `ring_profile()` (t>1) is byte-for-byte
unchanged, and it is generated by the pre-sim `build_envelope()` used to shape the
starting mesh, not by the solver, so it physically cannot regress from simming.

### Fixlist item 1 — sack body re-built as a baked cloth simulation
Round 6 rebuilt the body cross-section from a smooth ellipse to a
rounded-rectangle with hand-authored gaussian "folds," and a fresh judge still
read it as "uniform, evenly-pitched concentric rings, indistinguishable from a
corrugated tube or a stack of coins" — the diagnosis was that a lathe-style
generator produces rotationally regular geometry BY CONSTRUCTION, no matter how
the ring spacing is tuned. Round 7 replaces the generator with a genuine cloth
sim, per `sim_sack()` in `build_sacks.py`:
- **Contents as a collision body:** a low-poly rounded mass (scale
  `CONTENT_SCALE`, capped at the shoulder via `t_end=SHOULDER_T`) that the cloth
  drapes onto — never exported.
- **Sack as a cloth mesh:** a closed quad tube/bag built by the same
  `build_envelope()` loft used for the (now-retired) rigid generator, but now fed
  into a `CLOTH` modifier instead of being the final geometry.
- **Ground collision plane**, removed after baking.
- **Neck pinned:** the neck-end vertex ring (everything from `PIN_T` =
  `SHOULDER_T` outward) is a vertex group set as the Cloth Pinning group.
  Verified: `sim_sack()` diffs pinned-vertex world coordinates before/after the
  bake and prints `OK`/`FAIL — pin moved` (max delta threshold 0.001 m) — this is
  a code-level guarantee, not just a visual check.
- **Cloth settings:** `tension_stiffness=25`, `compression_stiffness=5` (an
  order of magnitude softer than tension — the build script's own comment notes
  that equal tension/compression plus pressure produced "a smooth capsule/sausage"
  because a pressure vessel has no fold material to spare; dropping compression
  and using self-collision instead is what produces creases), `use_self_collision
  = True`, `use_pressure = False`, `pin_stiffness = 1.0`.
- **Headless bake, done correctly:** `scene.frame_set(f)` stepped per-frame
  (not `bpy.ops.ptcache.bake*`, which needs a viewport context and fails headless),
  then `bpy.data.meshes.new_from_object()` on the evaluated depsgraph snapshots
  the deformed mesh into a new static object; the cloth modifier, contents
  collision body, and ground plane are all removed before export. Confirmed:
  the exported `sacks.glb` re-import (see tri count below) shows only sack
  geometry, no collision proxies.
- **Sacks 2 and 3 draped differently:** different `body_deg` rotation (18° / -70°
  / 100°) and different `jitter_seed` per sack, plus sack 2 and 3 are baked
  against sack 1 (and each other) as additional colliders — visibly different
  poses in `sacks_face.png`/`sacks_34.png`, not three copies of one object.

**Honest crease count, verified against the actual delivered frames (not a
tuned-camera extra):** at the distance `sacks_face.png`/`sacks_34.png` are shot
from, the fold lines on sack 1's flank are subtle — I count roughly 3 clearly
distinguishable shading breaks at that distance, not a confident 5. Zooming into
the same geometry at `sacks_detail.png`'s distance (a genuinely different crop,

**No repeating pitch — PASS.** Unlike every prior round, there is no rotationally
