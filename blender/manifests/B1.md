## market (L1) — sandbox/assets/models/market.glb — PASS
**What it is:** a Victorian glass-and-iron covered market hall, open-sided between
brick piers, with a big arched glazed roof and a semicircular glazed screen filling
each gable end.
**Functional parts modelled:** joined (brick piers on a plinth, iron columns bolted
to base plates with a capital bracket where the truss lands, riveted hoop-style
lap joints throughout); supported (triangulated iron roof trusses — bottom tie +
rafter chords + verticals + zigzag diagonal web — carrying real glazing-bar roof
geometry); moves/opens (entrance gate posts with hinge-plate implication, gates
read open); fixed (piers on a continuous plinth, columns on base plates, downpipes
strapped to piers); weathered (soot-darkened brick, worn threshold); maintained
(gutters/downpipes route to ground, ridge ventilation lantern has its own roof).
**Round-3 fix (fixlist item 4 — gable evidence):** the semicircular glazed screen
sits in the gable wall plane at x = ±42 (X_HALF). `build_trusses()`'s station list
independently spanned the full -42..+42 range, so its two outermost stations also
landed at x = ±42 — exactly coplanar with the screen's radiating ribs. No camera on
either side of a coplanar pair can separate them, so option (b) "try one interior
backlit camera" was not attempted — it cannot work in principle when the members
share a plane. Applied path **(c)**, the pre-authorized minimal geometry exception:
`build_trusses()` now iterates over `COLUMN_XS` (the six interior bay stations,
already one bay in from each gable) instead of recomputing its own end-to-end
range, so no interior truss shares the gable's x-depth. This is a one-line
station-list fix, not new geometry — the gable's own screen and the interior
trusses were already separately modelled; they were simply occupying the same
plane by accident.
**Provenance:** `market.blend` re-saved 17:20, `market.glb` re-exported 17:23, all
six render frames re-rendered 17:21-17:23 (all postdate the blend). The prior
builder's 16:03-16:05 frames (including its own `market_interior.png` /
`market_gable.png`) were overwritten in this pass — they no longer exist on disk.
**Regression check:** compared the new `market_34.png` (17:21) against the old one
(16:03, no longer on disk, examined before the rebuild). The only visible
difference is the disappearance of the end trusses that used to sit flush in the
gable wall; the arcade, roof pitch, ridge line, and column rows are otherwise
identical — consistent with a station-list fix and not a wider edit.
**Renders:** face → `renders/market_face.png` · three-quarter →
`renders/market_34.png` · detail (entrance gate/hinge/threshold) →
`renders/market_detail.png` · context (pulled-back, required >4m) →
`renders/market_ctx.png` · interior (trusses+columns from inside) →
`renders/market_interior.png` · **gable** (semicircular screen close-up) →
`renders/market_gable.png`
**Stranger's three answers, from `market_gable.png` alone:** What — a glazed
semicircular screen with radiating iron ribs, filling the peak of a market hall's
gable end, springing from the eave line to the ridge. How built — the arc is its

## market-stall — sandbox/assets/models/stall.glb — PASS
**What it is:** a collapsible trestle market stall — two A-frame trestles, a
plank counter, and a canvas awning tied to two poles, holding produce crates.
**Functional parts modelled:** joined (cross-halved A-frame legs pegged at the
crossing with a shaped iron collar bracket + washer plates, tied to a stretcher
rail); supported (canopy poles socketed into a clamp collar + backing plate +
bolt nubs at the trestle head, not a bare cube); moves (A-frame crossing implies
the fold joint); gripped (stretcher rail doubles as a hand rail); fixed (canvas
tied to the pole heads, real produce crates sitting loose on the counter, not
melted into the boards); weathered (canvas sags in a continuous catenary between
the poles, not flat panels).
**Round-3 fix (fixlist item 2):** two sub-items.
1. *X-crossing peg framing* — was already correctly re-aimed by the prior
   (dead) builder's 10:58 edit; `stall_detail.png` shows the shaped bracket
   clamping both legs with visible flanges, fully in frame. No further change
   needed; re-rendered fresh for provenance.
2. *Tie wraps at pole heads* — the prior builder's fix (three stacked flat
   cylinders + one diagonal beam) was NOT sufficient: a dedicated close-up
   render (`stall_polehead_check.png`, since deleted as scratch) showed it
   reading as three machined washers/nuts on the pole, not rope lashing. Ran a
   full rebuild: `add_rope_wrap()` now builds an irregular bunched-canvas
   gather (6 tapered lobes around the pole, not a perfect ring), a two-turn
   helical coil of short beam segments standing proud of the gather (reads as
   wound rope, catching light on real edges instead of a smooth torus), and a
   loose hanging tail with a knot bulge at the departure point. Verified with a
   Workbench `--quick` pass before committing to the Cycles pass.
**Tri count:** 2,008 (well under the 8k prop budget — canvas rope wraps are cheap;

positively evidence "IN FRAME" per the fixlist's literal wording. Detail frame

**Black triangular void in `stall_detail.png`:** checked — this is the shadowed
gap between the two crossing legs where the stretcher rail passes below the
peg, not a missing face. Confirmed by inspecting geometry directly (the
stretcher rail beam and both leg beams are present and closed at that location).
**Provenance:** `stall.blend` re-saved 17:13, `stall.glb` re-exported 17:15, all
four renders 17:14-17:15 (all postdate the blend).
