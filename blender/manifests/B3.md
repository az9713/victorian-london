## Asset 4: gaslamp.glb

Stranger line: "A cast-iron Victorian street gas lamp — a lamplighter could
rest a ladder against the crossbar and open the lantern door to light the
jet inside."

Fixlist item 3 (REBUILD): the lantern floor pass-through (black blob beside
the burner pipe) and the frame corners (black dots); add the vent-cap
louvre gaps and photograph them.

This was the most-changed asset this round. Found on arrival:
`gaslamp.blend` (19:37:49) was newer than `_detail.png`/`_vent.png`
(19:33 — stale, from BEFORE the vent-cap fix), while `_face`/`_34` were
fresh (19:37-19:38). I re-ran the full script rather than patch just the
two stale frames, then found and fixed two more real problems the r2 fix
attempt hadn't caught, working iteratively against full-resolution
pure-black pixel counts (not eyeballing):

1. **Vent-cap void (the fixlist's louvre-gap ask).** The "recessed core"
   cylinder linking the 3 proud collar rings used `radius=HALF` at a 45°
   vertex offset — but for a 4-sided cylinder, radius is the VERTEX
   distance, not the flat-face (apothem) distance (apothem = radius·cos45°).
   That left the core's flat faces 0.044 m short of the lantern's own rim,
   an uncovered gap straight down into the unlit lantern interior —
   2,404-2,762 pure-black pixels in `gaslamp_vent.png` depending on which
   stale/fresh version. Fixed with a sealed square plate across the full
   lantern-top opening, sized to the rim exactly, BEFORE any of the
   ring/core geometry starts — independent of the vertex-vs-apothem math
   above it. Result: 0 pure-black pixels in `gaslamp_vent.png`, and the 3
   proud collar rings with real open steps between them (the louvre) are
   now clearly visible and lit.
2. **Lantern floor pass-through (the fixlist's exact "black blob beside the
   burner pipe").** The floor plate had a 0.05 m-radius square hole for the
   gas feed pipe, but the pipe itself started exactly at the floor's OWN top
   face — nothing occupied the hole through the floor's 0.02 m thickness, so
   it was a genuinely empty, unlit recess. Extended the jet pipe down through
   the full floor thickness (a real gas feed rising from below) and snugged
   the hole to the pipe's radius (0.05→0.026 m). Result: 0 pure-black pixels
   in `gaslamp_detail.png` (was 25-99 samples depending on iteration).
3. **Frame-corner dots — not fully closed by the prior round's fix.** After
   fixing (1) and (2), a small (99-pixel) pure-black sliver remained next to
   the door latch — a genuine geometric overlap between the latch box and
   the corner post it sits beside (same volumetric-overlap class as the
   earlier rail fix, not yet applied to the latch). Flush-mounted the latch
   against the post's outer face instead of overlapping it by 0.006 m.
   Result: 0 pure-black pixels.
4. All 4 frames (`face`, `34`, `detail`, `vent`) re-rendered together in one
   script run after each fix, so provenance is clean end to end
   (blend → glb → all 4 renders, single run, 20:41-20:42) and the GLB in
   `sandbox/assets/models/gaslamp.glb` matches exactly what's photographed.

**Honest deviation:** the sealed cap plate adds 0.02 m to the lamp's overall
height (2.40 → 2.42 m) — a rounding-scale change to close a real hole, noted
here rather than silently absorbed.

Renders: `blender/renders/B3/gaslamp_face.png`, `_34.png`, `_detail.png`,
`_vent.png`. Tri count: 5,728 (prop budget, under 8k).

**My three stranger answers from the frames alone:**
1. What is it — `_face.png` / `_34.png`: a fluted cast-iron column with a
   stepped base and a square glazed lantern on top, with a horizontal
   crossbar partway up — reads as a Victorian gas street lamp.
2. How is it used — `_detail.png`: the lantern door is open on a hinge with
   a latch/catch on the frame it swung away from, and a gas jet/burner pipe
   is visible rising through a real floor pass-through inside.
3. How does it vent — `_vent.png`: 3 stacked proud collar rings with genuine
   open steps between them sit under a pyramidal cap — a real louvre, not a
   smooth cone.

**Verdict: PASS.** All three sub-items of fixlist #3 (floor blob, frame
corners, vent louvres+evidence) closed and confirmed at 0 pure-black pixels
across all 4 frames; louvre gaps photographed in a dedicated `_vent.png`.
