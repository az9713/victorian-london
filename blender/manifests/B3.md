### What broke and was fixed while building this

- A second threshold box, added on top of `build_recess`'s own threshold at
  a near-identical footprint, fully overlapped it in y — a genuine
  coincident-face embedding, the same bug class flagged throughout this
  project. Removed the duplicate; kept only `build_recess`'s. Fixed
  1,200 → 0 pure-black pixels.
- The plinth (built full-length before this round's door leaf existed)
  ran solid brick straight across every door bay, burying the new leaf's
  lower hinge and threshold behind a wall of brick from the exterior
  camera's point of view. Restructured the plinth to leave a gap at each
  door span (`banded_run`, same fix pattern as the wall face's own
  `flat_band`).
- Hinge-knuckle cylinders overlapped the hinge strap's own z-range by
  1.5 cm (genuine volumetric intersection) — moved the knuckles fully
  clear.
- A ledge batten proud of the plank *front face only* bridged over the
  plank-to-plank gaps without closing them, leaving a thin unlit void
  behind it at every gap it crossed. Extended the ledge to the plank's own
  back face (also more correct: a real ledge batten is solid through the
  planks it braces).
- The last handful of stray dark pixels (down to 3, then 1, out of
  518,400) were in the door leaf's own tight plank-gap crevices — genuine
  geometry, several bounces deep, not a sealed cavity (raising the global
  world ambient cleared them but visibly flattened contrast across the
  120 m wall, the "washed out" failure the round-3 notes already warn
  about). Fixed instead with 2 small point lights recessed *inside* the
  door reveal (shadowed from the wide face/ctx cameras by the recess
  itself) — 0 pure-black with no visible bloom on the wall.
