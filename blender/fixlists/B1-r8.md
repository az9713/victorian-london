## 1. sacks (2) — the folds exist, they die at viewing distance
This is NOT another shifting description. The judge's finding is mechanical and
it names its own cause:
- at sacks_face.png / sacks_34.png — the wide viewer-height frames a player
  actually judges from — it counts **0-1 faint shading transitions with a real
  cast shadow** on the nearest sack. A stranger says "smooth lumpy pillows or
  dough".
- zooming to sacks_detail.png DOES show sharper creases. So the simulation
  produced real folds. They are being lost between the sim and the wide frame.
- a second, separate defect: the creases that survive radiate from one pinch
  point in an **evenly-spaced fan** — regular and converging, which reads as
  folded-paper faceting rather than irregular cloth crumpling. Same uniformity
  trap as the ring generator, arriving by a different route.

### The cause, and the number that proves it
**sacks.glb is 5,088 tris against an 8,000 budget. 2,912 triangles are unspent.**
The r7 brief warned that decimating a simulated mesh can smooth the folds back
out, and that is what the frames show. The fix is not more simulation cleverness.
It is to stop throwing the folds away and to spend the budget that is sitting there.

### Do this
a. **Decimate far less.** Raise the decimation ratio until sacks lands near
   7,500-7,900 tris — under 8,000, but only just. Re-render and re-count after
   every change to the ratio, never before.
b. **Increase fold amplitude across the MID-BODY panels**, not at the neck.
   Lower compression stiffness further, and/or add localised pinning noise so
   panel creases form away from the tie and survive at low poly. The body is
   what fails; the neck already passes.
c. **Break the radiating fan.** Creases must not all converge on one pinch point
   at even spacing. Vary their origins, lengths and depths so no two match, and
   so at least two run diagonally across the body rather than radially from the
   tie. If you can see a rhythm, it has failed again.
d. Drape sacks 2 and 3 differently from sack 1 so the pile is not three copies.

### The measurement that decides the round — do this before claiming anything
Open the DELIVERED sacks_face.png and sacks_34.png and count the creases that
have their own cast shadow ON THE NEAREST SACK, at that frame's own distance.
Report the number. The target is 5 or more. A count taken from sacks_detail.png
does not count — that is the crop where the folds already pass. Round 7's
manifest claimed ~3 at wide distance and an independent judge counted 0-1, so
count conservatively and report what you actually see.

Honest stranger sentence for sacks_face.png, again: seven rounds have produced
