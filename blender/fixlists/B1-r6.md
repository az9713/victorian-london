## 1. sacks — REBUILD THE BODY MASS (score 2, the batch minimum, do this first)
This is a rebuild, not an adjustment. Keep only: the ground-contact scallop
creases (they PASS) and the neck's smooth non-faceted tip (it PASSES).

A filled jute sack is a flat-woven cloth tube, filled, so it bulges between
CREASES and sits with CORNERS, not as a smooth ellipsoid. Countable in
sacks_face.png, sacks_34.png and sacks_detail.png:

a. BODY SILHOUETTE: each sack must have flat-ish panel areas, not one continuous
   curve. Count 3 or more distinguishable planar-ish facets per sack, separated
   by shading breaks visible in clay.
b. CORNER PUCKERS: a filled sack has corners where the woven seams run. Count
   2 or more corner puckers per sack at the base, proud enough to cast shadow.
c. CLOTH FOLDS: count 5 or more fold or crease lines running across the body of
   sack-1, each with its own shadow line. Currently the only creases in the whole
   asset are at the ground contact.
d. SHOULDER: the body must narrow to the neck ABRUPTLY, at a distinct shoulder
   line, not by gradual taper. The judge read the current taper as "a tapering
   limb or tail". Countable: a silhouette kink where body becomes neck.
e. CORD BAND (this is the r5 leftover, now in its proper context): a band
   clearly NARROWER than the body and WIDER than the taper directly above it,
   with a shadow on both sides. It must be countable as a separate ring, not a
   smooth swelling.
f. FLARED END above the band, wider and blobbier than the pinch. (This already
   passes — preserve it.)

The stranger test the judge will apply to sacks_face.png: "that is a pile of
filled cloth sacks". If the honest answer is "dough" or "a curled animal", it
has not passed, whatever the neck looks like.
