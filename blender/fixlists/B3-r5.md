## THE TRAP IN THIS ROUND — read before you touch a camera
The judge's own summary: this batch is "bottlenecked by render/evidence choices,
not by missing geometry." Four of the six items below look like framing problems.

That is HALF the truth, and taking only that half will fail round 6.

If a feature is modelled but does not register in a frame shot at viewer height
and standing distance, there are two possible causes and you must decide WHICH:
  (A) the camera never showed it — a framing defect. Fix the frame.
  (B) it is built too shallow, too thin, or too small to read at that distance —
      a GEOMETRY defect that a tighter crop merely hides.
A previous round of this batch had two arch-ring orders built at 0.08 m and
0.18 m proud on a 13.3 m arch. They existed. They rendered as one flat face.
Cropping closer would have "proved" them and the object would still have been
wrong at the distance a player actually stands.

So for EVERY item below: first judge at viewer distance whether the feature is
deep/thick/large enough to read. If it is not, INCREASE THE GEOMETRY, then frame
it. Deliver both the wide frame where it must read and the crop that counts it.
A crop alone, on a feature that fails at standing distance, is not a fix — it is
the defect wearing a disguise.

Bar: every asset >= 4. Zero pure-black. Every render postdates its .blend.
Re-export touched GLBs. Rewrite blender/manifests/B3.md.

## 1. gy-flank (3) — the wall surface, both causes at once
The judge: face.png and ctx.png are "~90% flat grey wall with a tiny door and
near-invisible openings — a stranger cannot identify the wall from these two
frames alone. Only the dedicated crops rescue it." The coursing break registers
as one faint line; the two-tier plinth as a thin white band.

a. DEPTH FIRST. The coursing break and the plinth are almost certainly cause (B).
   Increase how far both stand proud of the wall face until each throws a shadow
   band readable in gy-flank_face.png at full wall width — not in a crop, in the
   WIDE frame. Countable: the coursing line and both plinth tiers each show a
   distinct shadow edge running the wall length.
b. DOWNPIPE. gy-flank_pipe.png shows a bare shaft, one bracket, the second cut
   off, and no discharge shoe. A 7 cm pipe is genuinely sub-pixel at wide
   distance, so this one is partly framing — but a downpipe on an 1880s wall is
   nearer 10-11 cm with a wider hopper head. Thicken it to a real diameter, add
   the hopper at the top, then reshoot so ONE frame contains: hopper, both
   brackets, the full run, and the shoe discharging at the ground.
c. A wall a stranger cannot read at wide distance is failing the bar even with
   perfect crops. After (a), reshoot _face and _ctx. The test: name three things
   a person does at this wall, from the wide frame alone.

## 2. viaduct-module (3)
a. PIER NICHE. Claimed stone surround and projecting sill; the judge sees "a
   small dark rectangle with no discernible frame or sill" in BOTH face.png and
   _34.png. Decide (A) or (B) honestly — if the surround is under ~5 cm proud it
   is (B). Build it proud enough to cast its own shadow, give the sill a real
   projection with a drip underside, then evidence it in a named crop
   viaduct-module_niche.png AS WELL AS leaving it readable in _face.
b. CROWN JUNCTION. viaduct-module_detail.png "shows what reads as overlapping /
   interpenetrating blocks near the ridge rather than a clean joint — this needs
   a second look for a possible geometry fault, not just a lighting issue."
   INVESTIGATE THE GEOMETRY FIRST. Check for coincident or interpenetrating
   voussoirs at the crown in the .blend, not in the render. If blocks overlap,

   re-shoot from a clearer angle, and then say in the manifest how you proved it.
   Do NOT touch the arc solve, the springing math, or the tiling.

## 3. gaslamp (3) — the vent, third round on this item
gaslamp_vent.png "fails its one job: the ribs read as a decorative stepped crown
or gear shape, not as a countable opening with shadow inside." This item has now
failed in r2 (absent), r3 (shallow steps), r4 (proud ribs, still reads as a cap).

Three rounds on one feature means the approach is wrong, not the execution.
Stop adding ribs to a solid cap. A vent is a HOLE. Build actual openings: cut
gaps clean THROUGH the cap wall into a hollow, unlit interior, so each gap shows
a dark void behind it rather than a shaded step. Countable in gaslamp_vent.png:
4 or more openings, each showing interior darkness distinctly darker than every
lit surface around it (dark, but NOT pure black — it must still be evidenced).
The stranger test: "hot air escapes through those holes."

Note for the manifest: gaslamp dropped 5,728 → 4,644 tris in r4 while gaining a
collar, braces and mullions. Report what was simplified to pay for that, and
confirm nothing that previously passed was lost.
