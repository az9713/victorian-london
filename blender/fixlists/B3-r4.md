## Method — read this before touching Blender
The r3 lesson, again: a builder checks "does it read as X?" and sees intent, not
pixels. So every item below is a COUNTABLE property. After rendering, verify each
numbered property is literally visible and countable in the named frame. If you
cannot count it in the render, the fix is not done — iterate before delivering.
Code that looks correct is not evidence. On r3 the viaduct's two arch-ring orders
existed in code at 0.08 m and 0.18 m proud on a 13.3 m arch and rendered as one
flat face. Depth in code is not depth in the frame.

Zero pure-black regions anywhere. Every render postdates its .blend. Re-export
every touched GLB to sandbox/assets/models/. Rewrite blender/manifests/B3.md.

## 1. gy-flank — WORST ASSET, do this first (score 2)
r3 judged it a bare flat plane for most of its length with props glued on, and a
door recess that is an empty rectangular hole. The barred window PASSES (4 rods,
rounded caps, socketed into the reveal, on a sill) — do not touch it.

a. DOOR LEAF in every door recess. Countable in gy-flank_detail.png at a crop
   that shows the whole opening:
   - the leaf fills the opening and sits BACK in the wall thickness, with a
     visible reveal shadow on at least two sides;
   - 4 or more vertical planks (or panels) with countable gaps or bead lines
     between them;
   - 2 or more hinges on one edge, each with 2+ countable knuckles;
   - one handle or latch, proud enough to cast its own shadow;
   - a threshold/step at the foot, proud of the wall face.
b. WALL SURFACE across the long bare spans. Countable in gy-flank_face.png and
   gy-flank_ctx.png:
   - a horizontal coursing break line (string course or brick-bond change)
     running the wall length, proud enough to cast a shadow;
   - a plinth at the ground line, proud of the wall face, with its own top
     weathering slope;
   - at least one more downpipe run, held by 2+ countable wall brackets, with a
     shoe at the bottom discharging to the ground.
c. Reshoot _face and _ctx after the rebuild. The test the judge will apply: name
   three things a person does at this wall. A blank slab fails it.

## 2. rookery (score 3)
The boarded window PASSES CLEAN (4 discrete planks, real gaps, backing shadow) —
do not touch it. The failures are windows and hardware.

a. SASH WINDOWS. Countable in rookery_face.png and rookery_detail.png:
   - a full glazing-bar grid, 6-over-6 or denser — count 12+ panes per window,
     bars proud enough to cast shadow, not one off-centre L-shaped bar;
   - a sill that PROJECTS past the wall face far enough to throw a visible
     shadow line under it (target >= 40 mm proud), with a drip/throat underside;
   - a lintel or arch head above the opening, proud of the wall face.
b. FRONT DOOR hardware. Countable in rookery_detail.png:
   - 2 or more hinges, each with 2+ knuckles (r3 shows one hinge-like blob);
   - a knob or lever, and a separate keyhole/escutcheon — two distinct objects;
   - a step or threshold at the foot.
c. YARD PRIVY. Countable in rookery_yard.png:
   - a roof with a slope and an overhang that casts a shadow on the wall below;
   - a vent — a gap, slot or louvre at the top, countable as an opening;
   - a door with at least one hinge and a latch.
d. STANDPIPE. Countable in rookery_yard.png: a turning handle a person could
   grip — a cross-handle or lever with real thickness, not a nub — plus the
   spout pointing down. A stranger must be able to say "that is a tap".

## 3. viaduct-module (score 3)
Do NOT touch the arc solve, the springing math, or the tiled joint — they pass.
The two-order ring is now visibly proud; that fix worked. What is left:

a. VOUSSOIRS. Countable in viaduct-module_face.png and _detail.png: the arch
   ring divided into 15 or more discrete wedge blocks, with radial joints
   between them recessed enough to read as joints in the frame. A smooth
   continuous band reads as an extruded profile, not masonry.
b. PIER-FACE RECESS. The small square notch in _face.png and _34.png has zero
   built detail and a stranger cannot explain it. Either give it a function —
   a framed inspection plate, a grille with 4+ countable bars, or a moulded
   blind niche with a sill — or remove it entirely. A blank notch is not a
   choice, it is an omission.
c. The dark wedge at the ring/crown junction in _detail.png: the r3 sweep says
   the darkest pixel there is 220, so it is shadow, not a hole. But it READS as
   a seam. Reshoot that crop with a fill light that lifts it, or adjust the
   geometry so the junction does not present a wedge. Do not just re-assert the
   pixel value — the judge is reading the picture.
